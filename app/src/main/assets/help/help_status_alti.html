Altimeter Status

This section provides several diagnostic screens to check the operational health and status of your altimeter.

Available Information & Tools

  • Altimeter Telemetry & Diagnostics: Displays hardware information such as altimeter type, firmware version, deployment channel continuity, current battery voltage, and real-time altitude.
  • Output & Actuator Testing: Allows manual testing of pyrotechnic deployment outputs or servo positioning.
  • GPS Coordinates (AltiGPS): For GPS-enabled flight computers, an additional dashboard displays detailed coordinates, heading, and distance between your mobile device and the rocket.
  • Live Map Tracking (AltiGPS): Displays an interactive satellite map showing the real-time positions of both your phone and the rocket for post-flight recovery.
⚠ CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING
Use the output test feature with extreme caution. Always ensure that no pyrotechnic charges or e-matches are connected to the board while performing tests on the ground.
