Select Bluetooth Module

You will be prompted to choose a Bluetooth module type if you have not selected one already or if you want to connect to a different altimeter.

Pairing & Selection Procedure

  1. Pairing: First, pair all your Bluetooth altimeter modules in your Android device's Bluetooth settings (default PIN is usually 1234 or 0000).
  2. Selection: From the list of configured modules, select the one your altimeter is using.
  3. Connection: Leave the screen and connect again. If you have chosen the wrong module or the altimeter is off, the connection will fail.
⚠ IMPORTANT ADVICE
Make sure that you can clearly differentiate and rename your Bluetooth devices on your phone so that you always connect to the correct altimeter.
