Flash Firmware

First, you need to select the firmware. The firmware depends on the specific altimeter model you want to flash.

Then decide if you want to directly flash the operational firmware or use a recovery firmware first.

Recovery Firmware: Use a recovery firmware if the configuration is corrupted and the altimeter can no longer connect. After loading a recovery firmware, you will need to re-flash the operational firmware.
⚠ CAUTION
Flashing the wrong firmware will result in an altimeter not working at all or not behaving as expected.

Required Hardware

  • A USB to TTL serial cable to connect to your phone/tablet.
  • A USB OTG adapter (USB-C or Micro-USB depending on your Android device).
  • Matching JST connector cables.

Supported Altimeters

Please carefully examine the pictures below to identify your hardware:

• AltiDuo (Large version)

• AltiServo

• AltiMulti

• AltiMultiV2

• AltiMultiSTM32 or AltiGPS

• AltiMultiESP32

• AltiMulti ESP32 accel

• AltiMulti ESP32 accel 345

• AltiMulti ESP32 accel 375

• TTGO BearAltimeter

Microcontroller Flashing Instructions

Alternatively, you can attempt to automatically detect the firmware.

1. ATmega328 Based Altimeters (e.g. AltiDuo, AltiServo, AltiMulti, AltiMultiV2)

  • Connect the USB TTL cable to the altimeter.
  • Select the correct firmware or auto-detect it.
  • Press and release the reset button on the altimeter, then tap the [Flash Firmware] button.

2. STM32 Based Altimeters (e.g. AltiMultiSTM32, AltiGPS)

  • Connect the USB TTL cable.
  • Select the correct firmware or auto-detect it.
  • Move the BOOT0 jumper to the right (programming position):

  • Press the reset button on the board.
  • Tap the [Flash Firmware] button.
  • Firmware should now be loading. When complete, move the BOOT0 jumper back to the left (run position):

  • Press the reset button to restart the altimeter in normal mode.

3. ESP32 Based Altimeters (e.g. AltiMultiESP32, AltiMultiESP32Accel variants)

  • Connect the cable.
  • Select the correct firmware or auto-detect it.
  • On the board, press and hold the BOOT button.
  • Press the RESET button and release it while continuing to hold the BOOT button.
  • Release the BOOT button:

  • Tap the [Flash Firmware] button.
  • The firmware should now load (retry if it fails). Once complete, press the reset button.

4. TTGO BearAltimeter

  • Plug in the USB-C cable directly.
  • Select the TTGO BearAltimeter firmware.
  • Tap the [Flash Firmware] button. When complete, press the reset button.

5. TTGO Mini, Simple Altimeter & TS-ESP32-S3

  • Plug in the USB-C cable.
  • Press and hold the BOOT button on the board.
  • Press and release the RESET button while still holding the BOOT button.
  • Release the BOOT button and select the correct altimeter model.
  • Tap the [Flash Firmware] button. When complete, press the reset button.
⚠ IMPORTANT NOTE
Never disconnect the cable during flashing. Doing so will corrupt the firmware image and require re-flashing from scratch.
